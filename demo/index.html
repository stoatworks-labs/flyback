Photosensitivity warning

This page flashes. Flyback’s sparks are flashes of light. The Tesla coil bangs many times a second (BPS sets how many, 5 to 1000, and the page opens on it at 120), the Van de Graaff sparks a few times a second, the Jacob’s ladder’s arc breaks and restrikes, and Fire makes a bang at once. In the Over build, Illumination lights the whole clip with each bang, so the clip flashes too. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, do not use this page.

The singing coil (Pitch Source, in the Singing group) bangs at the note’s rate, 5 to 1000 times a second, and starts and stops with the music: a melody of short notes is a flash with every note, this page’s own test tune included. Turned down with Octave, or on a low note, the rate can fall below the frame rate, and then every bang is its own flash. Pitch Source is off until you turn it on.

Broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom’s, and WCAG’s) puts the line at three flashes in any second. How often this page’s machines flash has not been measured yet. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
